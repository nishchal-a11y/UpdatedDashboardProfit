Client clusters

Clients are grouped on how much revenue they book and how many delivered hours they take. Groups are lettered biggest first, so A is the top revenue group, then B, C and so on; a Zero revenue group holds clients with hours logged but nothing booked against them. The pill is the group's rate — revenue per delivered hour measured against the company average: High rate is 125% of average or more, Standard rate is near average, Low rate is 80% or less. So a high-rate group earns more per hour worked, and a low-rate group takes more hours to earn the same money. Click a card to filter the table below to that group.
